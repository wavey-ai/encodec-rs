# Quality Comparison

Status: provisional only.

The quality audit measures RMS error, RMS dBFS, SNR dB, SI-SDR dB, loudness, true peak, channel correlation, inter-channel balance, spectral distance, clipping, and seam residual.

These rows compare canonical PCM with the fork output through the current unpublished acv2 envelope. They are not Profile 1 evidence and do not replace the required listening approval.
